The rules. Top 150 by 60-day turnover, re-ranked monthly. A new position only when the equal-weight index of those 150 is both above its 50-day average and higher than ten trading days ago. The trigger is a first cross — today's close above yesterday's VAH (200-bar volume profile on the 10-minute chart) while yesterday's close was at or below the day before's VAH. Of the day's crossers, take the lowest 5-day delivery ratio. Buy at the close, ₹20,000, 8% stop, exit on the tenth trading day, two positions at most.

What the backtest said, including the bad part. 111 trades, +1.95% net per trade, about 28% a year on ₹40,000 — but 2023 and 2024 carry all of it and 2025–26 were flat while the market rose. 111 trades cannot prove an edge smaller than 2.09% per trade, and the measured figure is 1.95%. That is why this is on paper.
